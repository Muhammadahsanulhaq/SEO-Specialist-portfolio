About

Practical experience, not theory.

I got into SEO through an interest in marketing, and built my actual skills by managing real client websites — not just studying the theory. Over 2.5+ years, I've worked across technical SEO, on-page and off-page optimization, local SEO, e-commerce SEO, and analytics, for clients in the USA, UK, and Pakistan.

My approach is diagnostic first: I look at what's actually limiting a website's visibility — technical issues, content gaps, weak authority, or poor local presence — before building a strategy around it. Rankings are the signal; qualified traffic and leads are the point.

More recently, I've started applying AEO and GEO principles — preparing websites to be understood by AI-driven search tools like Google's AI Overviews and LLM-based assistants, alongside traditional search engines.
